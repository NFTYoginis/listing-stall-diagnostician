# Case 01 — expected

Minimum assertions. Wording will vary. Every assertion must hold.

## Must assert

- [ ] **Failure confirmed.** 47 days is identified as beyond the comp range (9 to 28).
- [ ] **Comparison-set integrity is stated**, and comp 6 is named as a limitation on
      account of the association and dues difference, or excluded from comparative-value
      reasoning.
- [ ] **Primary constraint is Stage 1, views.** Not showings, not offers.
- [ ] Subject views are compared against the comp range and identified as below the
      lowest comp, not below the average.
- [ ] **Primary cause is search threshold exclusion**, referencing $412,000 sitting above
      a $400,000 filter boundary while five of six comps sit below it.
- [ ] **Mechanism is stated separately from cause** and describes absence from filtered
      searches rather than the property being overpriced in value terms.
- [ ] **Downstream stages are explicitly declared uninformative.** The report must say
      that no conclusion is drawn about the gallery, condition, or walkthrough because
      too few buyers reached them.
- [ ] **Syndication is ruled out** using the even view distribution across the three
      portals.
- [ ] **Listing data error is ruled out** using the verified attributes.
- [ ] **Null is tested and rejected**, citing district median DOM holding at 14 to 16 and
      two comps listed late in the window still moving fast.
- [ ] **Absence of a prior reduction is noted** as no completed experiment being available,
      rather than passed over silently.
- [ ] Confidence is **Provisional** or **Supported**, with the reason tied to the comp-set
      limitation and the absence of impression data.

## Must not

- [ ] Does not name the two showing-feedback notes as evidence of a cause. Four showings
      is not a readable sample and the report should say so if it mentions them.
- [ ] Does not diagnose the hero image as primary. May list it as untested and live once
      visibility is resolved.
- [ ] Does not suggest a price, a reduction, or a target band.
- [ ] Does not rewrite the description or suggest gallery changes.

## Drift signal

If the constraint lands anywhere other than Stage 1, the starved-stage rule in `rules.md`
has stopped binding. That is the single most important thing this case guards.
