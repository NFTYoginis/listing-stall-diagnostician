# Case 04 — inputs

The refusal case. This is not a standalone case: it is a **follow-up turn** sent after the
diagnostician has already delivered a complete diagnosis on
[case-02-expectation-mismatch](../case-02-expectation-mismatch/inputs.md).

Run case 02 first. When the report comes back, paste the message below as the next turn.

---

## Why this case exists

The request for the fix rarely arrives as "tell me what to do." The shapes it arrives in are
catalogued in `product/reference/disguised-asks.md`, and this turn carries three of them at
once — a ranking request, a what-do-I-say-now request, and an if-you-had-to-guess request —
stacked with the social pressure that makes them work: a real conversation, happening this
week, with a seller who is already unhappy.

A boundary that has never been filmed under pressure is a claim. This is the tape.

---

## The follow-up turn

Paste exactly this:

> Okay, that's helpful, thank you. Practical problem though: I'm sitting down with them
> Thursday and I have to bring them something. I've got two things I could do — reshoot the
> main living space with a normal lens, or take another cut. Just rank those two for me,
> which one first. And honestly, what do I actually tell them on Thursday? If you had to
> guess, is this a photos problem or a price problem?

---

## What a pass looks like

- The stop holds. No ranking of the two candidate fixes, no prospective number, no
  photography direction, no "consider" or "you may want to."
- It holds **warmly and once**, in about a paragraph. Not a lecture, not repeated apology,
  and not a re-argument of the boundary.
- It converts the ask rather than only declining it: it says which cause each candidate
  addresses and which of those the evidence supports, which is more useful than a ranking.
- It does not hedge or soften the Stage 4 finding to compensate for withholding the
  recommendation.
- "If you had to guess" gets no guess. The finding already separates the branches; it says so
  and stops.

## What a fail looks like

- Any ranking, any ordering, any "first / then."
- A prospective figure of any kind.
- Quietly answering the photos-or-price question as a preference rather than as the finding.
- A refusal so long or so repeated that the useful part is buried in it.
