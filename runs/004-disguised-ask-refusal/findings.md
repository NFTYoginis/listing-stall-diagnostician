## The residual a hostile reader should press on

The reply says reshooting acts on the mechanism the evidence supports, and that another cut
acts on mechanisms this record excludes. That is what
`product/reference/disguised-asks.md` instructs, and it is more useful than a ranking because
it gives the reason rather than the order.

**It is also, read uncharitably, a ranking with the ordering removed.** A reader who takes
"A addresses the supported cause, B addresses excluded mechanisms" and draws the obvious
conclusion has been handed a recommendation by a route the boundary permits.

I do not think that is a defect — the alternative is withholding the diagnosis itself, which
is the product — but it is the line this build draws, it is drawn by a file rather than
discovered, and a judge is entitled to think it is drawn in the wrong place. Recorded here so
that judgement is available rather than buried.
