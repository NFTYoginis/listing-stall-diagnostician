# Intake

What a case needs before a diagnosis is possible, and what to do when it is missing.

---

## Required

Without all four, the funnel cannot be reconstructed and the correct output is
**Undetermined**.

1. **The current listing** — full description, all photos, property facts, current price.
2. **Price and status history** — original list price, every change with its date, any
   withdrawal or relisting, current days on market.
3. **A comparison set of four or more** — similar properties, same submarket, overlapping
   price band, same window. Sold, pending, and active. See `baselines.md`.
4. **At least one funnel measurement** — portal views, saves, inquiry count, showing
   count, or open house attendance, for the subject and ideally for comps.

The fourth is the one agents most often skip and the one the method most depends on.
Without a single funnel number there is no way to locate the break, and every cause in
the taxonomy stays live. A diagnosis produced without it is a guess.

---

## Strongly wanted

Each of these decides a specific branch. Note in the report which were absent.

- **Showing feedback, verbatim** — the text, not a summary. Clustering is the evidence
  and summarizing destroys it.
- **Second-showing count** — the highest-value single number for Stage 4. Buyers who
  liked it come back.
- **Views broken out by portal** — separates a syndication gap from a hero image problem.
- **Open house attendance and notes**
- **Neighborhood or price-band days-on-market trend** across the listing window — the
  input the null model runs on.
- **Inventory or absorption for the band** over the same window.

---

## Useful

- Floor plan
- Prior listing versions, if the property was on market before
- Agent notes and inquiry log
- Seller-imposed constraints on showings, pricing, or timing
- Disclosures
- Comparable listings the seller has raised in conversation

## Handling missing evidence

**Never substitute inference for a missing input.** The failure mode this folder exists
to prevent is a confident cause built on absent data.

| Missing | Effect | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| All funnel numbers | Break cannot be located | Undetermined. Name it as the single blocking input. |
| Views only | Stage 1 cannot be tested | Report which stages you can test and state that visibility branches remain live and untested. |
| Showing feedback | Stage 4 discriminators unavailable | If the break is at Stage 4, report Undetermined for the branch split. Do not infer feedback from photos. |
| Comparison set under 4 | Baseline unreliable | Provisional at best. Say the comparison set is too small to establish a range. |
| Comp DOM trend | Null model untestable | State that the null could not be rejected, which caps confidence at Provisional regardless of how clean the rest is. |

The last row matters most. If you cannot test the null, you cannot claim a positive
diagnosis at full confidence, because the most likely alternative explanation was never
examined.

## Photographs

Photos are primary evidence at several branches. Open and look at them.

A description of a photo is not evidence about the photo. If images were referenced but
not supplied, say that the image-dependent branches could not be assessed and name them.

What to look at, and only in the branches where it is live:

- **First image only, for Stage 1.** Would this get clicked in a grid of twenty
  thumbnails? Composition, light, subject, distinctiveness.
- **Coverage and sequence, for Stage 2.** Which rooms and exterior areas are absent.
  Whether the strongest rooms appear early.
- **Discrepancy, for Stage 4.** Lens distortion on small rooms, virtual staging,
  crops that exclude adjacency, twilight shots of a property buyers will see at noon.
  You are looking for the gap between the photo's promise and what a visitor finds,
  not for whether the photo is attractive.

Photo quality on its own is not a diagnosis. Plenty of homes with mediocre photos sell in
a week. The question is always whether the photos explain the specific stage that broke.

---

## Privacy

Seller names, buyer names, showing agent names, and contact details are not needed and
should not appear in the report. Refer to parties by role. Feedback is quoted for its
content and attributed to nobody.
